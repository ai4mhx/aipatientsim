named by its transcript identifier, decoded in the repository README:

  position 1  source     A ChatGPT-4o, B Gemini 2.5 Flash, C Claude Sonnet 4

so BMY3 is a geriatric male with schizophrenia generated by Gemini 2.5 Flash.

Transcripts of interviews with human participants (identifiers beginning with D)
are not included and will not be released. See the repository README.
